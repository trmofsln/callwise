# 15-minute retry assessment and prospective experiment

Recommendation: **keep the existing rule pending evidence and supplement it with verified eligibility and stopping constraints**. No data supports changing the production delay. The app's default 60-minute cooldown is a disclosed configurable operating assumption, not a measured replacement for the production rule.

The PDF describes 15-minute retries but explicitly leaves production configuration to organisers. ERP reporting code found a 15-minute bucket from hotlead creation; that is not the delay between consecutive unanswered calls. Reporting marts expose attempt/talk counters whose ETL/denominators/completion semantics remain unverified. In the capped pilot, 162 observed consecutive sampled pairs followed an unanswered status: two within 15 minutes, 54 within 60–120 minutes, 74 later within a day and 32 on later days. Sampling omits calls between these observations; these are not verified retry sequences or population frequencies. Completed-meeting outcomes and propensity overlap are unavailable, so no keep/change causal effect or calls-per-meeting estimate can be calculated.

After organisers confirm canonical outcomes, attribution, timezone, consent/DNC and operating constraints, run a shadow phase to verify coverage and latency. Then randomize eligible seller/lead chains within employee, vertical, local-day/time and history strata to the existing 15-minute policy or a frozen model policy. Keep identical maximum call budgets and common safety rules. Allocate by seller/chain to avoid treatment contamination across retries; record assignment probability, eligibility, proposed/action times, attempts, connects, scheduled meetings and matured completed meetings.

Primary analysis: intention-to-treat completed meetings per eligible seller at equal call budget, plus completed meetings per 100 attempts and calls per completed meeting. Report scheduled meetings and connects separately. Use seller-cluster uncertainty and prespecified major cohorts, await full outcome/verification maturity, and compute power from the real baseline prevalence before selecting a sample size. No fabricated power or baseline effect is supplied. A positive 95% lower bound and 10% relative yield improvement are aspirational acceptance criteria; otherwise keep the baseline and preserve the measured result. Review cohort degradation and repeated-calling complaints before any operational deployment.
