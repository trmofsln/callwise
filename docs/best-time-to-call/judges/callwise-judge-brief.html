Judge brief · product + evidence

The right hour to call, and what to do when nobody picks up.

IndiaMART sales teams place about 324,000 outbound click-to-call dials a day. Callwise tells a rep, for each seller, which future hour to call, how to retry after a miss, how confident it is, and why, in two or three sentences grounded in real history. The goal is completed meetings, not just answered calls.

What we built and proved A live recommender, retrained on 10× more data, with every claim tested on a sealed holdout.

Seller history first, a trained hour-by-weekday model when history is thin, hard calling rules always, and an LLM that may explain but never decide. We also tested whether richer ML picks better times. It predicts which calls convert, but not when, so we kept timing simple and designed a pilot to prove uplift.

02 · Decision engine

The most specific evidence wins, and rules always run first

Every recommendation walks the same ladder. A plan says which rung produced it, so a rep can see how much to trust it.

0

Calling rules gate

always · deterministic

10:00–18:00 Mon–Sat, 60-minute minimum gap, 3 attempts a day, 10 per 7-day sequence, do-not-call marks, booked appointments, 7-day horizon, at most 4 ranked windows.

1

Seller's meeting history

≥ 2 calls that led to meetings

Hours whose calls led to meetings rank first. A weekday's own slot is used once it has enough support; otherwise the hour is pooled across weekdays.

2

Seller's answer history

≥ 3 answered, one hour ≥ 2

Hours the seller has answered rank next. A call under 20 seconds counts less: priority × (1 − 0.75 × short-call share).

3

Completed-meeting model

waiting for verified labels

Slot reserved for a calibrated meeting_done model. It stays off until canonical labels exist, so no probability is ever overstated.

4

General timing model v4

150,875 calls · 723 meetings

Smoothed weekday × hour meeting rate with dev-fitted calibration. It covers all weekdays and backs off to the hour when a cell is thin.

5

Calling hours

last resort

With no usable evidence, the earliest allowed windows are offered and the plan says so plainly.

Retry logic

Simulated, not guessed

The engine adds a hypothetical unanswered call at the end of the recommended window, recomputes every rule and ranking, and takes the top result. Same-day results are a retry; later days are a postpone; do-not-call or exhausted caps are a stop. Delays of 15 min, 30 min, 1 h, 2 h and 1 day are evaluated and kept as hypotheses for the pilot.

Confidence

Tied to evidence, never to model scores alone

Moderate, low or insufficient, based on how much verified history backs the rung that ranked the window. No plan shows a meeting probability unless a calibrated, approved meeting model produced it.

06 · AI / LLM

The LLM explains; it never decides

GPT-6 Luna, through OpenRouter, reviews each plan with read-only tools and a schema that pins every field to the deterministic result. Any deviation falls back to the rule-written explanation.

Guardrails in code

lib/best-time-llm.ts

4 read-only toolsprediction context, the selected scorer, retry plan, validation
Enum-pinned JSONtool, model version, route and validation must equal the engine's output
Budget12 s deadline, ≤ 6 tool calls, 900 output tokens, no reasoning tokens
One repaira mismatched review gets one retry, then deterministic fallback
Untrusted inputsource data is labelled untrusted; no SQL, no training, no window changes
Secrets server-sideAPI key never reaches the browser, logs or prompts

Why this design

Product reasoning

Reps act on the time, not on prose, so the time has to come from measured evidence. The explanation is a 2–3 sentence factual template filled from that evidence; the LLM's job is to check it against tool outputs and confirm it is grounded. If the model is slow, wrong or unavailable, the rep still gets the same plan and an accurate explanation, labelled as rule-checked.

This keeps cost and latency bounded, makes every sentence auditable, and means an LLM outage can never produce a wrong call time.
